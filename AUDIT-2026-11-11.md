## 4. What the 93 "not indexed" pages are

GSC's per-URL list lives under **Indexing → Pages → "Why pages aren't
indexed"** (each reason row expands to the URL list and can be exported).
From the site's own data, the pool is composed of:

1. **Sold / rotated stock URLs (largest bucket).** 24 listings are currently
   delisted in the DB (12 early `AR7-26xxx` showroom placeholders + 12
   goo-net cars rotated out on 2026-11-05), and more have churned over the
   weeks. Their `/inventory/{stock}` URLs leave the sitemap the moment they
   are delisted, so Google parks them as "Crawled – currently not indexed"
   and later drops them. **This is correct behaviour for a dealer site** —
   sold cars are supposed to fall out.
2. **Fresh stock & model pages still queued** — "Discovered – currently not
   indexed". 130+ stock URLs carry lastmod 2026-11-05/07; Google indexes a
   young site's long tail gradually.
3. **Filter / parameter URLs** (`/inventory?make=Toyota`, body filters) that
   are linked from pages but canonicalise to `/inventory` — "Alternate page
   with proper canonical tag" / "Duplicate". Working as designed.
4. **Staff routes** blocked by robots.txt (`/crm`, `/account`, `/portal`,
   `/studio`, `/seo`, `/call`) — intentionally excluded.
5. **Redirect variants** (`www.` and `http://` forms) — "Page with
   redirect". Normal.

None of these buckets is an error state. The healthy signals to watch:
- the **green (indexed) line keeps climbing** toward the ~500-URL universe;
- the grey line stays roughly flat (it is the churn + queue, not a defect);
- destination pages, guides, news, model hubs — the evergreen money pages —
  are all in the indexed set (verified prerendered + canonical above).
