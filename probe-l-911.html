PORSCHE 911 Description

The Porsche "911" is a 2-door coupe type sports car whose sales began in 1964. This vehicle has consistently adopted the RR (rear-engine, rear-wheel drive) layout since its launch, making it a distinctive presence today. While minor changes have occurred over time, the basic form and round headlights have remained unchanged since its birth. In 2013, the special edition "911 50th Anniversary Edition" was released to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the "911." The model released in March 2018 includes the "911 GT3 RS" grade. It features wider side skirts and a front spoiler lip, generating powerful downforce. The interior uses black Alcantara and leather, accented with CFRP parts featuring a carbon weave finish. The information in this description is current as of September 2018.
