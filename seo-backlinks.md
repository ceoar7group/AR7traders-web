## What this list is, and is not

These are parties AR7 actually trades with — forwarders, port agents, brokers,
inspection firms, suppliers, insurers, chambers. A link from the company whose name
is on the bill of lading is worth more than a hundred directory submissions, and it
is the only kind this agent can honestly help you get.

No script can create a backlink: a backlink is somebody else deciding to link to you.
Anything that claims otherwise is buying links or spamming forums, both of which cost
more traffic than they gain.
